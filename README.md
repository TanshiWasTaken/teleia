# Teleia
#### semicolon's custom, all-in-one bot

Teleia is a multi-functional, advanced and modern discord bot for semicolon whose purpose is to help with moderation, accommodation, management, economics, entertainment and many more.

## Definition

el. **τέλεια** /ˈte·li·a/ (adj.)
: perfect

grc. **άνω τελεία**[^1] /ˈa·no te·ˈli·a/ (n. f)
: greek semicolon, upper point, raised point

[^1]: despite the different spelling, the English spelling (and bot's name) - Teleia - is supposed to mean both perfect and semicolon and be pronounced the same.

## Functionality

### 1. Moderation
<details>
<summary>Teleia offers automated, AI-powered moderation and user-friendly commands for a lighter weight on the staff team's back.</summary>

#### 1. Tweakable AI moderation for blocking inappropriate content
Teleia uses tweakable artificial intelligence to detect and block inappropriate content such as spam, NSFW, hate speech and more. The AI can be modified to block specific types of content.

---

#### 2. Tweakable AI moderation for censorship
Teleia also offers AI moderation for censorship, which can censor discussions of triggering or sensitive topics, words used in some contexts, warn users of a loud video/audio file etc.

---

#### 3. Intuitive and user-friendly commands for staff
Teleia provides intuitive and user-friendly slash commands for staff members to help them manage the server more efficiently without giving them any headaches or dread in doing so, thanks to the ease of use of its moderation commands.

---

#### 4. Strike system integration with designated database
Teleia includes a strike system that allows staff members to track user behaviour, issue warnings and pardon/punish users with strikes accordingly. The strikes are recorded in a designated database collection, permitting staff members to access and review user history through the bot.

---

#### 5. Automatic moderation
Teleia can also perform moderative actions based on user behaviour. For example, if a user receives a certain number of strikes, Teleia can punish the user according to the number of strikes the user has. This feature would help reduce the workload of staff members and ensure rule violations are dealt with consistently and constantly.

---
  
</details>

### 2. Accommodation
<details>
<summary>Teleia offers accommodation features to make users feel welcome and prevent them from getting overwhelmed.</summary>

#### 1. Welcome messages
Teleia will send a message welcoming each new member who joins the server. This message includes a greeting, a general guide on how to get set up on the server, and links to resources.

---

#### 2. Verification system
Teleia's verification system is a multi-step automated process with manual approval by staff members designated to prevent spam and protect the community from unwanted or malicious users. When new users join the server, they must complete the automated verification process before opening a ticket to talk with one of our staff members to get approved into the server.

---

#### 3. Self-assignable roles
Teleia's self-assignable roles feature allows users to choose which roles the users want to assign themselves, granting them greater control over their server experience. These roles may include preferred pronouns, interests, age group, and other criteria.

---

#### 4. Birthday database
Teleia keeps track of users' birthdays if they choose to set them and sends automated messages to celebrate their special day. This feature helps foster a sense of community and make users feel valued and appreciated since other users will see this celebratory message and wish the celebrated one a happy birthday.

---

#### 5. Feedback
Teleia's user feedback system allows users to provide feedback on the server and its features, which helps improve the server and enhance the user experience.

---
  
</details>

### 3. Management
<details>
<summary>Teleia offers a variety of management features to help server owners and staff members keep their servers organised and running smoothly.</summary>

#### 1. Server statistics and analytics
Teleia provides server statistics and analytics to help administrators understand more about the server. These include information on user engagement, text and voice channel activity, server growth over time, age groups etc. Teleia also provides insights into which commands' usage is the most frequent and which of the server's areas are most active. This information can help administrators make data-driven decisions to improve the server's performance and engagement.

---

#### 2. Scheduling
Teleia's scheduling feature helps plan and coordinate events and reminders. With this feature, staff members can schedule events and members can set reminders for themselves and get notified when events are coming up.

---

#### 3. Role-based access control
Teleia's role-based access control system allows administrators to control access to different server features, resources and channels based on roles.

---

#### 4. Integration with external tools and services
Teleia integrates with various external services, such as social media platforms, webhooks and API endpoints which allows seamless integration with other services, providing administrations with additional functionality and flexibility for managing the server.

---

#### 5. Ticket system
Teleia's ticket system helps streamline user requests and support issues by providing an automated way for users to open tickets and for staff members to manage and respond to those tickets.

---

#### 6. Logs
Teleia tracks and logs events such as user actions, moderation actions, and more to allow staff members to investigate incidents or disputes and maintain accountability for users, including staff members.

---
  
#### 7. Levelling system
Teleia's levelling system has its own designated database collection. It will keep track of the number of messages a member has sent to the server to assign them a level. Users can reach level milestones, and when they do, they win a prize. There is a leaderboard for this which ranks users based on their level.
  
---
  
</details>

### 4. Economy
<details>
<summary>Teleia offers an economy feature to institute a local currency system to keep users engaged and encourage participation. As well as to offer a medium of exchange for virtual goods and services.</summary>
  
#### 1. Currency system
Teleia's economy system includes a currency (token) earned by participating in activities, such as chatting, participating in events, or completing tasks assigned by staff members. Users exchange it for virtual goods and services offered by other members.

---

#### 2. Shop system
Teleia's economy system provides a shop where users can spend their earned currency on various items. These items can include but are not limited to custom roles, pieces of literature, drawings and whatever other users have decided to sell.
  
---
  
#### 3. Lottery system
Teleia's lottery system allows users to spend currency on lottery tickets. At set intervals, a random drawing takes place. The winner receives a prize, such as a large sum of tokens or a special reward.
  
---
  
#### 4. Leaderboard
Teleia's economy system includes a leaderboard, which ranks users based on their total earned currency.
  
---
  
#### 5. Logs
Teleia tracks all transactions and changes to user currency balances, allowing administrators to audit the system and investigate discrepancies to ensure the system is fair and transparent.
  
---
  
#### 6. Auction system
Teleia's economy system offers an auction system, allowing users to bid on items or services marketed by other users or the staff team, creating a sense of competition and excitement within the community and providing users with a new way to engage with each other.

---
  
</details>

### 5. Entertainment
<details>
<summary>Teleia offers various entertainment features to keep users engaged and entertained within the server.</summary>

#### 1. Games and activities
Teleia offers various games and activities such as trivia, would you rather, quizzes, truth or dare etc., as well as activities such as movie nights, stand-up comedy and any others that administrators come up with. Users who participate and win in activities will receive currency or a special reward.
  
---
  
#### 2. Music and radio
Teleia's music and radio feature allows users to play music within one of the server's voice channels. Users can spend an amount of currency for a playlist in which users can save their favourite songs and have a seamless listening session without needing to add each track every time. Teleia can also stream radio stations, allowing users to listen to their favourite radio stations from within the server.
  
---
  
#### 3. Virtual pets and games
Teleia's virtual pets and games feature allows users to adopt and care for virtual pets, play games and earn rewards within the server. Users can play chess against other users, and if they win, they gain tokens based on the difficulty their opponent gave them.
  
---
  
#### 4. Special events and challenges
Teleia can host special events and challenges from which users can earn rewards and prizes based on their contribution to the event or challenge.
  
---
  
#### 5. Clubs
The server's structure allows Teleia to engage with each club in unique ways that match the club's topic.

---
  
#### 6. Personality
Teleia uses artificial intelligence while generating responses to commands or auto-responders to give Teleia a personality.
  
</details>
